GOT QUESTIONS?

Quick answers.

What to know before you download.

Can I download both video and audio?+

Yes. Choose MP4 for video with sound or MP3 to extract audio. MP3 conversion requires FFmpeg on the server.

Why did a YouTube link fail?+

A video may be private, age-restricted, region-restricted, live, removed, or temporarily unsupported. Try a publicly accessible video. YouTube can change how its media is delivered, so availability is not guaranteed.

Does it work on mobile?+

The website is responsive and can be used from a mobile browser. Download behavior depends on your browser and device.

Can I download any video I find?+

Only download videos you own or have permission to save, and follow YouTube’s terms and applicable copyright law. YT Drop does not bypass private access or DRM restrictions.

Is YT Drop affiliated with YouTube?+

No. YT Drop is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube or Google.
